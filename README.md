 CalmPath - A Mental Health Support Web Application

CalmPath is a full-stack mental health platform designed to make therapy and emotional wellness accessible to everyone. Users can book sessions with therapists, track their mood over time, journal their thoughts, and receive personalized wellness reports.

Live Demo
(https://calm-path-onlyfounders.vercel.app)

Features

Therapist Booking — Browse and book sessions with licensed therapists directly through the app
Free Trial — First 3 sessions are completely free for new users
Mood Tracker — Log your daily mood and visualize your emotional patterns over time
Mood History — View a complete history of your mood entries
Journaling — A private space to write and reflect on your thoughts and feelings
Wellness Report — Get a comprehensive report summarizing your mental wellness journey
User Authentication — Secure login and account creation

TechStack

Frontend - HTML, CSS, JavaScript 
Backend -  Node.js 
Database - JavaScript (database.js) 
Deployment - Vercel
